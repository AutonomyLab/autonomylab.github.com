Autonomy Lab / SFU Mountain Dataset


A dataset of woodland trail navigation on a ground-based robot in changing environmental conditions.


We apologize, but we are still evaluating solutions for hosting the SFU Mountain Dataset in its unabridged form. The full dataset consists of several hundred GB of sensor data including full video from 6 cameras, and range data from 2 laser scanners, plus calibration parameters for each sensor.

In the mean time, please feel free to download our ground-truth place matches: frames from each camera at locations separated by approximately 10 meters, hand-matched between dry, wet, dusk, and night traversals. Here is an example place match for each camera across all four sessions:
